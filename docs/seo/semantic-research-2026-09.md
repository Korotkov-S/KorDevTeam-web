# SEO research: commercial services

Research date: 2026-09-22; CRM, web-services and mobile-app SERPs refreshed 2026-09-23; AI-automation and support/development SERPs refreshed 2026-09-24. Search demand was checked in Yandex Wordstat for
Russia, all devices. Mobile-app demand uses the period 2026-08-23 through 2026-09-21; earlier clusters use 2026-08-22 through 2026-09-20. Wordstat numbers are
grouped query counts, not forecasts of unique qualified leads.

High-frequency informational phrases are used only where the page satisfies
commercial intent. For example, generic `разработка веб приложения` and
`разработка мобильных приложений` include learning and employment demand, so
the landing pages lead with qualifiers such as `на заказ`, `для бизнеса`,
`под ключ`, price factors and delivery process.

## SERP observations

The Yandex results for `разработка CRM на заказ` (region: Vladimir) repeatedly
use these elements on the strongest commercial pages:

- an exact commercial phrase in title and H1;
- `на заказ` or `под ключ`, followed by a concrete business scenario;
- integrations with 1С, telephony, email, sites and other systems;
- visible price or explicit factors affecting the estimate;
- delivery stages, contract/guarantee language and related cases.

The implementation therefore avoids doorway pages and places query language
inside complete service pages with problems, deliverables, integrations,
process, estimate factors, evidence, FAQ and a lead form.

The refreshed CRM SERP also repeatedly separates solutions by business
scenario: sales, service companies, production, branch networks, client
portals and industry platforms. Competitors explain the difference between a
custom and boxed CRM, ownership of source code and data, migration, MVP timing
and integrations. The CRM landing page therefore keeps one canonical owner for
the primary cluster and adds these medium- and low-frequency intents as useful
sections and FAQ rather than generating overlapping landing pages.

The refreshed results for `разработка веб-сервисов на заказ`, `разработка
личного кабинета` and `разработка B2B-портала под ключ` show one closely
connected commercial intent rather than three unrelated products. Strong pages
describe concrete user scenarios, roles and access, orders, payments,
documents, integrations with 1С/CRM/ERP, delivery stages and cost factors. The
`/services/web-services/` page therefore remains the single canonical owner for
this cluster and exposes separate crawlable sections for solution types,
business fit, integrations, MVP, ownership of code and B2B portal scenarios.
This avoids thin overlapping landing pages while giving medium- and
low-frequency queries a precise answer inside the commercial page.

The mobile-app cluster has a large informational and educational head, while
the exact commercial phrases are much smaller. The page therefore leads with
`разработка мобильных приложений для бизнеса`, uses `на заказ`, iOS and Android
in the title, and answers the lower-frequency intents around cost, 1С/CRM/ERP
integration, offline work, publication and MVP in dedicated sections and FAQ.
It also explains when an adaptive site, PWA or Telegram Mini App is sufficient,
so the landing page helps the buyer choose a format instead of forcing every
task into a native application.

The integration cluster is led by `интеграция 1С и Битрикс24`, but the buyer's
actual task is broader than one pair of products. Russian commercial results
and implementation cases repeatedly describe a fragmented landscape of CRM,
1С, ERP, sites, warehouses, telephony and external services. The page therefore
keeps the exact 1С/Битрикс24 phrase in the SEO title while positioning the
service as integration of corporate systems into one end-to-end digital
contour. Supporting intents such as `интеграция CRM с 1С`, `интеграция сайта с
1С`, `настройка обмена 1С и Битрикс24` and `интеграция интернет-магазина с 1С`
are answered inside the same canonical landing page rather than split into
thin overlapping pages.

  protects the exchange when one system becomes unavailable —
  <https://habr.com/ru/articles/905706/>.
- YogurtStudio's 1С and Битрикс24 case treats 1С as the authoritative source of
  accounting data and covers the full sales cycle from customer records and
  inventory to shipment and sale —
  <https://workspace.ru/cases/integraciya-1s-i-bitriks24-dlya-predpriyatiya-stimul/>.
- FATFOX's warehouse case connects the site, CRM and 1С so reservations and
  stock remain current across sales channels; the reported operational effect
  is fewer warehouse errors, returns and overtime —
  <https://workspace.ru/cases/avtomatizaciya-raboty-sklada-internet-magazina-kofeynyh-kapsul/>.

The AI-automation cluster mixes broad informational demand with commercial
requests for implementation. The page therefore uses `внедрение ИИ в
бизнес-процессы` as its human-readable H1 while retaining the observed
commercial language `AI-автоматизация бизнеса`, `под ключ`, `AI-ассистент`,
`корпоративная база знаний` and integrations with 1С/CRM in metadata, sections
and FAQ. It does not position a generic chatbot as the product. The buyer is
shown a controlled operational scenario: a repeatable information task,
available examples, measurable quality, a pilot on real data, human approval
for critical actions and monitoring after launch.

Russian implementation and market evidence used for the AI page:

- Sber's LegalDocs team describes fact extraction from legal documents,

  <https://habr.com/ru/companies/sberbank/articles/874062/>.
- NeuroB2B's implementation page combines AI agents and corporate knowledge

The support cluster contains several different buying situations: an incident
that requires a quick response, takeover of somebody else's code, regular
maintenance, and continuous product development. Wordstat confirms that buyers
more often use `техническая поддержка сайта`, `доработка сайта` and
`сопровождение сайта` than the narrow phrase `поддержка веб-сервиса`. The page
therefore uses the former phrases in metadata, headings and FAQ while retaining
web-service development as the commercial proposition for more complex
products. Generic `развитие сайта` has a very noisy grouped head, so its small
phrase count is treated as supporting vocabulary rather than a standalone SEO
page.

The landing page also separates the two kinds of work instead of mixing them in
one unstructured list. Incidents are handled through triage, diagnosis,
stabilisation and prevention; planned development goes through a backlog,
testing and controlled releases. The three internal cases demonstrate this
range: Sims Dynasty Tree is a continuously developed paid web product; Jully
Bride combines malware cleanup, vulnerability remediation and a reduction in
database requests from roughly 1,500 to 9; WoWBanner combines calculators,
technical SEO and gradual development of an internal CRM.

The delivery model is now visible as part of the commercial proposition rather
than hidden behind a generic technology list. Every support client receives a
public Kanban board for tasks, statuses, questions and history; the team records
progress daily, agrees the report and next plan on Mondays, and exposes the
public board through MCP so the client can ask a compatible AI assistant such
as ChatGPT for the current project state. This block supports conversion and
trust rather than introducing a separate search landing page.

## Search-engine requirements applied

- Google Search Essentials: people-first useful content, searcher language in
  prominent elements and crawlable links —
  <https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials>.
- Google JavaScript SEO: meaningful status codes, unique metadata, canonical in
  original HTML and SSR/pre-rendering where practical —
  <https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/javascript/javascript-seo-basics>.
- Google Core Web Vitals targets: LCP at most 2.5 s, INP below 200 ms and CLS
  below 0.1 at the 75th percentile —
  <https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/core-web-vitals>.
- Yandex site quality: useful query-matching landing pages, headings,
  descriptions, Schema.org, accessible images, human-readable URLs and pages
  available to the crawler — <https://yandex.ru/support/webmaster/en/site-quality-index>.
- Yandex regionality: regional commercial queries depend on consistent service
  geography and verified company information —
  <https://yandex.ru/support/webmaster/en/site-geography/site-region>.

## Implemented content decisions

- One canonical page owns each search intent; no near-duplicate city or keyword
  pages are generated.
- Titles, descriptions, H1 and lead copy use the commercial qualifiers supported
  by demand, while the body explains the service for a human buyer.
- Every service links to relevant published cases, articles and concise FAQs.
  Links are server-rendered and available without JavaScript.
- Service pages emit `Service`, `BreadcrumbList` and `FAQPage` structured data
  in addition to the shared organization and page entities.
- All published service URLs remain in the pages sitemap.

## Measurement after production release

Track impressions, clicks and average position by landing page in Yandex
Webmaster and Google Search Console. In analytics, compare organic sessions with
`form_open`, `form_start`, `form_submit_success`, email and Telegram goals.
Evaluate qualified leads rather than traffic alone. Review query-to-page fit
after the first meaningful impression sample; refine snippets and sections on
the existing canonical URLs instead of creating overlapping pages.
